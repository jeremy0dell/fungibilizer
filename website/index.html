The Key Insight

The ability to fung is itself an asset — scarce, transferable, and consumed on use.

The Fungibilizer token is an ERC-721. It grants its holder the right to fung exactly one NFT. Upon use, it is permanently burned. One Fungibilizer, one funging, then it ceases to exist.

This creates a two-market structure: a market for the act of transformation (Fungibilizer tokens) alongside a market for the transformation's output (pool tokens).

The market prices the act itself.
